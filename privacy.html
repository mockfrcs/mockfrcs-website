Privacy Notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version. We collect your email address, your exam activity and, if you buy a membership, a record of your payment. We use it to run your account and improve the questions. We do not sell your information and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. Who is responsible for your information

MockFRCS (mockfrcs.co.uk) is operated by [COMPANY NAME] Ltd, company number [COMPANY NUMBER], registered office [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]. We are the controller of the personal information described here, and we are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under reference [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER].

For anything to do with your information, email hello@mockfrcs.co.uk.

2. What we collect

  • Launch updates. If you ask to be notified when MockFRCS opens, we hold your email address.
  • Your account. Your email address and password. The password is stored in encrypted form by our login provider and we cannot read it.
  • Your membership. Which plan you have and when your access starts and ends.
  • Your exam activity. The answers you choose, questions you flag, your scores, the time you take, and your answers in the practice question bank.
  • Feedback you give us. If you leave a review: your star rating and comments, and, if you choose to add them, a display name and training grade, and whether you allow us to publish the review.
  • Early access applications. If you applied for early access, the details you gave in your application.
  • Payments. When you buy a membership, our payment provider collects your card details. We receive confirmation of the payment and the plan bought. We never see or store your card number.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we keep the correspondence.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, the servers that host MockFRCS record basic information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, any information about patients.

3. Why we use it

What we doOur legal basis
Create your account, give you access to what you bought, save your answers and show your resultsTo perform our contract with you
Take payment and keep financial recordsContract, and our legal obligations
Email you when MockFRCS launchesYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link
Publish a review with your display nameYour consent, given when you submit it
Look at how questions perform across all users so we can find and fix poor questionsOur legitimate interest in improving the service
Keep the site secure and prevent account sharing or copying of contentOur legitimate interest in protecting the service
Reply to your emails and deal with complaintsOur legitimate interest in supporting our users

We do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not use your information for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the companies that provide the services MockFRCS runs on, and only so far as they need it:

  • Supabase stores our database and runs account login.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Mailchimp holds the launch notification list and sends those emails.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser contacts Google's servers to load them, which shows Google your IP address.

Some of these companies process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

  • Launch updates: until you unsubscribe, or until we stop sending them.
  • Your account and exam activity: while your account is open, and for up to 12 months after your membership ends in case you return. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.
  • Payment records: for as long as the law requires us to keep tax and accounting records.
  • Emails: for up to two years after the matter is closed.

You can ask us to delete your account sooner at any time.

6. Cookies and similar storage

MockFRCS does not use advertising or analytics cookies. When you log in, your browser stores a small piece of information so that you stay signed in. This is necessary for the site to work. Our payment provider may set its own cookies during checkout to process the payment and prevent fraud.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to stop or limit how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • ask for your information in a form you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw any consent you have given.

To use any of these, email hello@mockfrcs.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

8. Age

MockFRCS is for surgical trainees and is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant we will email account holders.

See also our Terms & Conditions.
